Starting a business in Quebec: sole proprietor or corporation?

By Meissa, Premier Client · Laval, Quebec · Updated November 20, 2026

The difference between the two, who has to register, what it costs, and where to start without paying for nothing. General information, not legal or tax advice. For your situation, talk to an accountant, a notary or Revenu Québec.

Free checklistPDF with the official links.

In short

Start with the CRA business number: it's free and you get it online right away. Sole proprietor under your own name: no mandatory Registraire registration. Under another name: $41, within 60 days. Quebec corporation: $397.

1. Sole proprietorship or corporation?

Sole prop.Corporation
Separate legal personNoYes
Gains and lossesYoursThe corporation's

A sole proprietorship has no separate legal personality: you keep the gains and bear the losses. A corporation is a separate legal entity with its own obligations (Québec.ca, in French). Regular Registraire fees: sole proprietorship and corporation; priority rates cost more. Québec.ca recommends talking to a lawyer or notary and an accountant before choosing.

Leaning toward a corporation? See incorporation costs and steps (Quebec Inc. or Canada Inc.).

2. Who must register with the Registraire?

  • Sole proprietor using a name that doesn't include your first and last name: required, within 60 days of starting.
  • Sole proprietor under your own name: not required.
  • Quebec corporation: registered when it's incorporated.

3. Start with the free step: the CRA business number

The CRA business number (BN) has nine digits. Request it with Business Registration Online; since November 3, 2025 phone registration is no longer accepted, and the number is issued right away. There is no cost to get one (Alberta.ca). You need a BN if you incorporate or need a CRA program account (CRA).

4. Where you actually save

  1. Under your own name, you skip the $41 Registraire fee.
  2. The CRA number is free: no need to pay someone just to get it.
  3. Quebec's Démarrer une entreprise service (in French) guides you step by step for free.

Or pay a service to do it

Online services can handle registration or incorporation for you. You pay for the time saved, so compare their price with the government fees above. The CRA business number is free and takes a few minutes to request yourself.

5. After registering

  • Annual update declaration every year.
  • Report any change (address, name, activities) within 30 days.
  • If you close: a striking-off declaration.

Next step: the CRA number and GST/QST registration.

FAQ

Does a sole proprietor have to register in Quebec?

Only when operating under a name that doesn't include their first and last name. They then have 60 days to register ($41 regular fee).

How much does it cost to incorporate in Quebec?

$397 at the Registraire's regular rate ($595.50 priority), then $106 a year for the annual update.

Is the CRA business number free?

Yes. You get it online with Business Registration Online, right away.
