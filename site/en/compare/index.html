Systeme.io or Shopify: which one should you start with?

Updated November 17, 2026

In short: if you sell your knowledge or services, start with Systeme.io. If you sell physical products, start with Shopify.

Systeme.io: free planShopify: free trial

See Systeme.ioSee Shopify

Pick Systeme.io if…

  • you are a consultant, coach, trainer or freelancer;
  • you want a sales page, an email list and checkout without stitching three tools together;
  • your starting budget is close to zero.
See Systeme.io

Pick Shopify if…

  • you sell items that ship or get picked up;
  • you need inventory, variants, taxes and calculated shipping;
  • you also plan to sell in person.
See Shopify

Plan prices and limits change often. Always check the official pricing page before you subscribe.
