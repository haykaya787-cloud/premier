Shopify taxes in Quebec: setting up GST and QST

By Meissa, Premier Client · Laval, Quebec · Updated November 20, 2026

When you must register, which rates apply, and where to enter your numbers in Shopify. General information only: for your situation, check with Revenu Québec or an accountant.

Try ShopifyFree trial; terms on Shopify's site.

In short

Under $30,000 in taxable sales, registration is optional. Above it, register, then enter your numbers in Shopify › Settings › Taxes and duties. Shopify calculates; you file.

1. Do you have to register?

If your taxable sales stay at $30,000 or less in a calendar quarter and over the four previous calendar quarters, you are a small supplier and registration is optional. Count worldwide taxable sales, yours and your associates' (Revenu Québec, in French). Above it, you must register for GST and QST and start collecting them.

2. Register voluntarily?

You can register below the threshold to recover the taxes you pay on business purchases, but you must then stay registered for at least one year.

3. The two taxes

GST (federal)5%
QST (Quebec)9.975%

Rates from Shopify's Canadian tax reference.

4. The settings in Shopify

  1. Settings › Taxes and duties
  2. Choose Canada
  3. Sales tax › Collect sales tax
  4. Pick the region you're registered in and enter your number
  5. Repeat for the other tax

Path from Shopify Help.

5. Check with a test order

Place a test order to a Quebec address: GST and QST should appear separately on the receipt.

Three traps

  1. Charging taxes before you're registered.
  2. Forgetting the other tax.
  3. Thinking Shopify files for you: it calculates, you file.

FAQ

What is the GST/QST registration threshold?

$30,000 in taxable sales in a calendar quarter and over the four previous quarters. Below it you are a small supplier and registration is optional (Revenu Québec).

What is the QST rate?

9.975%. GST is 5%.

Does Shopify register me for GST and QST?

No. You register with the government, then enter your numbers in Shopify › Settings › Taxes and duties.
