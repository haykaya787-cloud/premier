What we built on the free plan

  1. Account. Free sign-up, no credit card.
  2. Domain. We authenticated premierclient.ca (Settings › Emails › Domains). Systeme.io gives the DNS records; on Cloudflare it took about ten minutes.
  3. Form. First name, email, an unticked consent box and a button, embedded on this site with one line of code.
  4. Welcome email. One automation rule: form sign-up → send email. The postal address and unsubscribe link live in the email footer settings.
  5. Test. Two test sign-ups: contacts arrive with their source page and form.

What surprised us: on a new account, our first automated email had not gone out after about ten minutes. Systeme.io reviews new accounts' first sends, which can take from minutes to a few hours (Systeme.io help). Plan for it before a launch.

Pros and limits

Pros

  • A real free plan, no credit card
  • Pages, email, checkout and courses in one place
  • Unlimited emails, even on the free plan
  • No transaction fees added by Systeme.io
  • French and English interface

Limits

  • Only one automation rule on the free plan
  • Free plan shows a "Made with systeme.io" badge on forms
  • Few native integrations; Zapier is sometimes needed
  • Less design freedom than a site builder
  • Quebec rules are yours to set up

What users say

On Capterra Canada, Systeme.io is rated 4.8 out of 5 across more than 2,100 reviews (see reviews, November 2026). Praise centres on simplicity and value; criticism on integrations and design freedom.

In Quebec: what to set up yourself

  • Consent: Canada's anti-spam law requires clear consent; the box must be unticked by default.
  • Postal address: every commercial email must say who you are and how to reach you.
  • Law 25: publish a privacy policy and the name of the person in charge.
  • Taxes: above the small-supplier threshold you must collect GST and QST. See our GST/QST guide.

Who is it for?

Yes: consultants, coaches, trainers, freelancers and authors testing an offer on a near-zero budget. Less so: stores with many physical products, who are better served by Shopify. Unsure? Read our comparison.

FAQ

Is Systeme.io really free?

Yes. The free plan needs no credit card and includes 2,000 contacts, 3 sales funnels and unlimited emails, according to the official pricing page checked on November 20, 2026.

Can I use Systeme.io in Quebec?

Yes, but you set up the local rules yourself: email consent, a postal address in every email, a Law 25 privacy policy and, above the small-supplier threshold, GST and QST.

Systeme.io or Shopify?

Systeme.io to sell a service, coaching or a course. Shopify for a store of physical products with inventory and shipping.
