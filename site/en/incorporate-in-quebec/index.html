Incorporating in Quebec: Quebec Inc. or Canada Inc.?

By Meissa, Premier Client · Laval, Quebec · Updated November 21, 2026

Official fees for both options, the steps one by one, and the CRA's role. The CRA doesn't incorporate businesses: federally it's Corporations Canada, in Quebec the Registraire des entreprises. General information, not legal or tax advice. For your situation, talk to an accountant, a notary or Revenu Québec.

In short

If you only do business in Quebec, a Quebec corporation costs less: $397 to start, versus $597 for a federal corporation that must also register in Quebec. Federal mainly pays off if you want your name protected across Canada.

1. Official fees (regular rates)

Quebec Inc.Canada Inc.
Incorporation$397$200 online
Registration in QuebecIncluded$397
Total to start$397$597
Every year$106$106 + $12

Sources: Quebec corporation fees and fees for corporations not constituted in Quebec (in French), federal regulations, Schedule 5, Corporations Canada. Priority: $595.50 in Quebec; federal express adds $100.

2. Steps: Quebec corporation

  1. Name: a number followed by "Québec inc.", or a proposed name after checking it isn't already used. Optional reservation: $27, valid 90 days.
  2. Articles of incorporation through the Registraire's online service.
  3. Pay $397: you get the certificate and the NEQ.
  4. Initial declaration (head office, directors): free if filed on time.

3. Steps: federal corporation

  1. Use Corporations Canada's Online Filing Centre.
  2. Get a numbered name ("12345678 Canada Inc.") or propose a name for review (how to get a name).
  3. Give the registered office address (no P.O. box) and first directors. This information is public.
  4. Pay $200 online (+$100 for express).
  5. If you do business in Quebec, register with the Registraire: $397.

4. Where the CRA fits

A federal corporation automatically gets its business number and corporation income tax account. A Quebec corporation has to register with the CRA for them, using the corporation name, certificate number and date, and jurisdiction (CRA). Next: GST and QST with Revenu Québec.

5. Every year

  • Annual update with the Registraire: $106.
  • Federal corporation: annual return to Corporations Canada, $12 online.
  • The corporation files its own income tax return.

Or pay a service to do it

Online services can handle registration or incorporation for you. You pay for the time saved, so compare their price with the government fees above. The CRA business number is free and takes a few minutes to request yourself.

FAQ

How much does it cost to incorporate in Quebec in 2026?

$397 with the Registraire des entreprises for a Quebec corporation (regular rate), then $106 a year. A federal corporation is $200 online plus $397 to register in Quebec, then $106 + $12 a year.

Does the CRA incorporate businesses?

No. Corporations Canada handles federal incorporation and the Registraire des entreprises handles Quebec. The CRA then assigns the business number: automatically for federal corporations, on request for Quebec ones.
