Shopify: the online store built in Canada

Updated November 17, 2026

Founded in Ottawa, Shopify is the best-known platform for opening an online store without writing code.

Try Shopify freeFree trial to build the store before you pay.

What it does

  • Store: catalogue, variants, inventory, themes.
  • Payments: cards and built-in checkout.
  • Shipping and taxes: calculated rates and settings by province.
  • In-person sales: a point-of-sale terminal tied to the store.
  • Apps: an extension store for almost anything.

Who it suits

Makers, retailers and brands that sell objects. With more than a handful of products, a real store saves you time from the first month.

Who it suits less

People who only sell a service or a course. A monthly plan plus paid apps is heavy when you have a single offer: see Systeme.io.

Where to start

  1. Use the trial to build the store before paying.
  2. Publish five products with good photos.
  3. Set up taxes and shipping for Quebec.
  4. Place a test order before announcing the opening.
