Shopify in Quebec: review and guide 2026

By Meissa, Premier Client · Laval, Quebec · Updated November 20, 2026

The online store platform born in Ottawa, seen from a Quebec business: what it does well, what apps add to the bill, and the three local settings not to miss.

Try ShopifyFree trial; terms on Shopify's site.

Our verdict

The safest choice if you sell products you ship. Inventory, payments, shipping and Canadian taxes are built in. If you sell a service or a course, Systeme.io is cheaper to start.

Transparency: we don't run a Shopify store. This guide relies on Shopify's and Quebec agencies' official documentation, linked in each section.

What Shopify does

  • Online store: themes, product pages, hosted cart and checkout.
  • Management: inventory, variants, orders, customers.
  • Shipping: zones, rates and local pickup.
  • Canadian taxes: Shopify calculates GST, HST, QST and PST once you enter your registration numbers (Shopify Help).
  • Apps: an app store for reviews, subscriptions, newsletters and more.

Pros and limits

Pros

  • Built for selling products end to end
  • Canadian taxes handled in settings
  • Admin and storefront available in French
  • Sell online and in person in one place

Limits

  • No permanent free plan: a trial, then a subscription
  • Paid apps add up
  • More setup than a simple sales-page tool
  • Often too much for a service or a course

The 3 Quebec settings

French first: your commercial site must be available in French, with any other language no more prominent. GST and QST: above $30,000 in taxable sales you must register and collect them; see our GST/QST in Shopify guide. Law 25: publish a privacy policy and the title and contact details of the person in charge.

Where to start

  1. Start the trial and set French as the default language.
  2. Add three products with photos, prices and weights.
  3. Set shipping (Quebec, rest of Canada).
  4. Turn on payments and, once registered, GST and QST.
  5. Publish your privacy policy and place a test order.

FAQ

Does Shopify calculate QST?

Yes, once you're registered and have entered your numbers under Settings › Taxes and duties. Shopify calculates; you remain responsible for filing.

Does Shopify have a free plan?

No. Shopify offers a trial, then a paid subscription. Trial terms change; check Shopify's site.

Shopify or Systeme.io?

Shopify for physical products you ship; Systeme.io for a service, coaching or a course.
